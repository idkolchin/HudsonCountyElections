HUDSON COUNTY ELECTION MAP RECORDS
Jersey City 2013, 2017, 2021, 2025  |  Hoboken 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2025

CONTENTS
  maps/         133 PNG images - one per map view
  candidates/   34 PNG images - one per candidate who ran in more than one year
  all_results.csv  every candidate result on record (295 rows, 76 contests)

MAP FILE NAMING
  <Town>_<Year>_<Race>[_Ward<X>]_<view>.png
    _leader  district map coloured by who led, shaded by their vote share
    _grid    small multiples - one mini-map per candidate, shaded by that candidate's share
    _runoff  the runoff round of that contest, where one was held
  Each image includes the title, controls, legend and full results summary as shown in the
  interactive tool. Basemap tiles are omitted so district colour reads cleanly.

CANDIDATE FILE NAMING
  <town>_<name>.png - votes by election, vote-share trend, and a table of every
  election that person appears in (office, round, votes, share, finish, result).

SOURCES AND VALIDATION
  2025 figures are validated line-by-line against the Hudson County Clerk's official
  "Results by Municipality, Ward and District" canvass (last updated 11/20/2025); all 77
  candidate totals for 2025 reconcile exactly. Earlier years come from the county's
  district canvass workbooks. Totals include mail-in, early and provisional ballots.

NOTES
  - Vote share is a candidate's share of votes cast in that contest, so it is not
    comparable between race types; an at-large field of 15 splits the vote far more
    than a two-way runoff. Vote counts are the like-for-like measure.
  - Board of Education contests are excluded.
  - Jersey City candidate identity is keyed on surname where the source gives no first
    name. These surnames appear in more than one year but are NOT treated as the same
    person, either because the source confirms different people or because identity
    could not be verified: Healy (Jerramiah Healy, 2013 Mayor, vs Catherine Healy,
    2025 Ward D - father and daughter), Reyes, Richardson, Wintner.
  - Hoboken name variants are merged where they are clearly one person
    (Jen/Jennifer Giattino, Jim/James Doyle, Dave/David Mello, Ruben J./Ruben Ramos Jr.).
